What to do

  1. Choose how you will do your Competency Study Guide, in the Choose one box below: the printed worksheet, or your own paper. Pick two pens you can tell apart.
  2. Read the Week 8 competencies first, so you know what you are looking for.
  3. The walkthroughs, the written notes and the Competency Study Guide all open Monday, October 26 at 8:00 am.
  4. Work through the four walkthroughs in order, because each one builds on the one before it. The order is the autonomic nervous system, then how hormones work, then the hypothalamus and pituitary, then reproductive hormones. The last one is the longest, so leave the most time for it.
  5. Each walkthrough has a worksheet in the right-hand column. Every time it asks you to predict, type your prediction there first. Show me stays locked until you do, and Next stays locked until you press Show me. On many steps the figure asks you to click something, like a nerve to fire or a hormone to release, and Next stays locked until you do.
  6. After each walkthrough, read its written notes to fill in anything you missed.
  7. For each competency, pick prompt A or prompt B and draw it in the box in your first color.
