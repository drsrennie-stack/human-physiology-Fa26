Step 7. Lab, how muscle makes force

Time: 2 to 3 hours. Counts for: Graded. Investigate It, 25 percent of your grade across the term.

PhysioEx is back this week. You run Exercise 2, Skeletal Muscle Physiology, where you stimulate an isolated muscle and record its twitches, summation, tetanus, fatigue, length-tension curve and load-velocity curve. Then you tire out your own hand with a clothespin and compare, and one clinical case puts it all back into a real body.
