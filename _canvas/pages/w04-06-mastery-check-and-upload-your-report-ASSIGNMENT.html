Step 6. Mastery Check, and upload your report

Time: 35 to 50 minutes per try. Counts for: Turned in. No points, but it has to be turned in.

The Mastery Check tells you which competencies are solid and which need another round. Take it as many times as you like; each try is a fresh set of questions.

What to do

  1. Open the Mastery Check. It is set to Week 4 and 50 questions.
  2. An attempt counts as your Mastery Check when it covers one week, has at least 50 questions, tests every competency, and you score 80 percent or higher. The report says Met or Not yet for each of those.
  3. If it says Not yet, go back to Step 5 for the competencies it lists, then take it again. If you could not start a competency at all, go back to Step 3 for that one.
  4. When an attempt meets the standard, open the report and save it as a PDF.
