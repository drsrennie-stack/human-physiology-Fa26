Last week you worked two exam questions on video: for each one you spent 15 minutes building a model to prepare your answer, then 5 to 7 minutes teaching it. This week you check that work against the rubrics I use on the exam, then look closely at what it shows you about how you learn.

How to do it

  1. Open your two Exam practice quizzes from last week. Each one holds your video and the answer you chose. Your videos are also in your Studio library.
  2. Watch the 15 minutes of preparing in each video and check the model you built against its rubric. Pause the video on your finished whiteboard to check it. A point counts only if you clearly said or drew it.
  3. Check your two answers against the key.
  4. Watch the 5 to 7 minutes of teaching in each video and count the points you clearly made.
  5. Answer the analysis questions, then post.

The rubrics and the answer key

The rubrics and the answer key appear in Discussion 5 in Canvas on Monday, October 5, after the practice quizzes close.

On the exam, each question is scored the same way: your model and your teaching are worth 75 percent of the points for that question, and your answer is worth 25 percent.

Analyze it

Answer each one in two or three sentences.

  1. For each question, were you right, and how sure did you feel when you gave your answer at the end of your video? Say whether each one was sure and right, sure and wrong, unsure and right, or unsure and wrong.
  2. Where did your model first go wrong or leave something out? Name the exact step.
  3. Was that a gap, something you did not know, or a misconception, something you were sure of that is not true? How can you tell?
  4. Compare your model score with your presenting score for the same question. Did presenting it out loud show you anything that building the model did not?
  5. What will you do differently before the midterm because of this?

What to post

  • Your answers to the five analysis questions.
