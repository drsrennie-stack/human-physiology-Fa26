What to do

  1. Choose how you will do your Competency Study Guide, in the Choose one box below: the printed worksheet, or your own paper. Pick two pens you can tell apart.
  2. Read the Week 5 competencies first, so you know what you are looking for.
  3. Your Competency Study Guide and the written notes are ready Monday, October 5 at 8:00 am, so you can print them and get started. The walkthroughs open Tuesday, October 6 at 8:00 am; before then the lessons link shows a Get ready page.
  4. Open the Week 5 lessons and work through the six walkthroughs in order. Give spinal reflexes the most time, since it is the biggest topic of the week. The order is spinal reflexes, then sensory receptors and coding, then spinal pathways, touch and pain, then cerebrospinal fluid and the blood-brain barrier, then vision, then hearing, balance, taste and smell.
  5. Each walkthrough has a worksheet in the right-hand column. Every time it asks you to predict, type your prediction there first. Show me stays locked until you do, and Next stays locked until you press Show me.
  6. After each walkthrough, read its written notes to fill in anything you missed.
  7. For each competency, pick prompt A or prompt B and draw it in the box in your first color.
