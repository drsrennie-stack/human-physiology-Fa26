Step 7. Lab, the sensory and reflex lab

Time: 2 to 3 hours. Counts for: Graded. Investigate It, 25 percent of your grade across the term.

There is no PhysioEx this week, because PhysioEx has no sensory exercise. Instead you collect real data on yourself: reaction time, two-point discrimination, vision, hearing range and balance. All you need is a ruler, a paperclip, your phone, and one other person for the first test.

What to do

  1. Open the sensory and reflex lab and print all three pages. Print page 3 at 100 percent, with fit to page turned off, or the charts come out the wrong size. There is a bar on page 3 to check it with.
  2. Run each test on yourself and record your data in the tables as you go.
  3. These are demonstrations of how your senses and reflexes work, not eye exams, hearing tests or medical screens. If something you notice worries you, take it to a clinician.
  4. Photograph or scan all three pages and combine them into one PDF.
