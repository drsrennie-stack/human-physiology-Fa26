Step 7. Lab, hormones and the autonomic system

Time: 2 to 3 hours. Counts for: Graded. Investigate It, 25 percent of your grade across the term.

This week's lab has five short parts. You watch your own autonomic nervous system change your heart rate, run three PhysioEx endocrine activities and the ELISA, read an unknown off a standard curve, read one menstrual cycle from its hormone graphs, and work one clinical case.

What to do

  1. Choose how you will do the worksheet, in the Choose one box below: printed and filled in by hand, or typed on screen. Either way, open the lab page for the directions.
  2. Do the two heart rate tests at home (Part 1). Read the safety note first: if you feel dizzy, sit or lie down right away.
  3. Open PhysioEx from the Pearson link in Canvas. Run the three Exercise 4 activities named on the lab page (Part 2), then the ELISA activity in Exercise 12 (Part 3). Fill in each activity's boxes while it is fresh, with your actual numbers.
  4. Draw the standard curve by hand and read the unknowns off it (Part 3). Then work the hormone cycle sheet by hand (Part 4).
  5. Answer the clinical case and the three look back questions.
  6. Scan or photograph every page, or save the typed page as a PDF and add photos of your standard curve and both pages of the hormone cycle sheet, and combine everything into one PDF.

Your worksheet is graded once PhysioEx shows each activity complete. The PhysioEx Lab Report that Pearson makes is not the worksheet.
