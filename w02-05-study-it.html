Every day from Wednesday, about an hour a day

Now the note sheet closes and the material has to come back out of your head. A little every day beats one long session. Pick from these, and try more than one. The signaling pathways in particular are worth redrawing from memory until the order of steps is automatic:

  • Rx Cards. Spaced recall cards for Weeks 1 and 2 together, so Week 1 does not fade.
  • Draw it from memory. Redraw the G protein pathway or the fluid mosaic membrane on a blank canvas with nothing open, then check it.
  • Brain dump. Take a competency prompt cold, on paper.
  • Book problems. End of Chapter 3 and Chapter 6. Work them forward before you look, then backward from the answer.
  • Study With Me. Quiz each other on what each second messenger does and which enzyme makes it.
