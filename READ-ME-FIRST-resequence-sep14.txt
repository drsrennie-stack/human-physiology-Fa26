THE NEW FILE
  biol005-w02-transport-notes.html
  The physiology notes for Week 2 that did not exist. Across all four notes files
  in the repo, osmosis, endocytosis, exocytosis and phagocytosis appeared zero
  times each, so the second half of Week 2 had a book chapter and videos and
  nothing to read on the site. This covers diffusion and Fick, osmosis and
  tonicity, facilitated diffusion, the pumps, transport maximum, the whole
  vesicular set, and transepithelial transport, with five worked problems and
  all twelve competencies with their brain dump prompts at the end. It has a
  Print button now, so students can keep it on paper beside the note sheet.
