How this works

  1. Pick a week and press Spin. You get one competency and one prompt.
  2. Put the screen down. Do the whole thing on paper, from memory, with the book and your Competency Study Guide closed.
  3. Come back and press Check my work. You get the list of what the prompt asked for.
  4. Tick only what is actually on your paper. Be honest; the list is the whole point.
