Your week is a loop

Several of you weren't sure how to approach each week's material or in what order, so I have simplified the whole week into one page that shows exactly what to do and when.

Two things matter most. First, do every step, in the order it appears. Each step sets up the one after it, so skipping ahead is usually what makes a week feel confusing. Second, this is not a task list you finish from top to bottom. It is a cycle: you start with a preview, build your understanding, test yourself with the Mastery Check, and then go back and refine whatever needs more work. Most weeks you will go around the loop more than once, and that is exactly how it is meant to work.

What the colors and letters mean
  • Slate: previewing the chapter
  • Navy: learning it
  • Maroon: pulling it back out of memory
  • Gold: checking what is solid
  • White: showing what you know
  • WThis step has a worksheet
  • SYou submit this in Canvas
  • TYou track this all term

Where to go back into the loop

This page is not a checklist you go through once and call done. Most weeks, some competencies will need more work before they are solid, and that is normal. Going back into the loop is how you learn them, not a sign that something went wrong.

The Mastery Check shows you which competencies need more work. Where you go back in depends on what each one needs. Open the one that sounds like you to see what to do next.

  • I could not start it at all.
    Back to learning

    Go back to the interactive slides or a video for that competency and add what you find to its box on your note sheet, in your second color. Then study it again.

  • I recognized the answer, but I could not draw it from memory.
    Back to retrieval

    Do a Brain Dump on that competency: blank paper, nothing open, draw it, then check it. Then run its Rx Cards.

  • I missed a step or a term.
    Back to retrieval

    Run the Rx Cards for that competency, then retake the check.

Retake the Mastery Check as many times as you want. It carries no points and no penalty, so its only job is to give you useful information: which competencies are solid and which ones need another trip around the loop. Going around the loop two or three times is not falling behind. It is the course working the way it was built to work.

Do the check before the discussion, because the discussion asks what your check showed and what you did about it.

Your patient, tracked all term

You follow two patients this term, one in each half. Every week you add that week's numbers to your patient's chart and note what you think is going on. You are not turning the chart in each week. You are building it.

  • Patient 1 (T1), Weeks 1 to 7

    Track your first patient every week. At Midterm 1 you hand in your analysis of this patient, and the midterm asks you a question about them.

  • Patient 2 (T2), Weeks 8 to 15

    You get a new patient for the last eight weeks. Track them the same way, and at the end of the term you hand in your analysis of this patient.

Your patient work counts for the same share of your grade as before. It is now split evenly between the two patients.

The same week in words

  1. Preview. Pre-read: look through the chapter's figures and bold terms and fill in the pre-read worksheet, so you know where everything is. 30 minutes at most. Has a worksheet. You submit it in Canvas.
  2. Learning. First pass: work through the interactive slides and fill in your note sheet in one color. 3 to 4 hours, in two or three sittings. Has a worksheet: your note sheet, which you submit at the upload step.
  3. Learning. Second pass: go through the same interactive slides again and add to the same boxes in a second color. 30 to 60 minutes.
  4. Learning. Upload your note sheet, both passes in one file. About 10 minutes. You submit it in Canvas.
  5. Retrieval. Study it with cards and brain dumps, in 4 sessions of 30 to 45 minutes spread over several days.
  6. Checking. Take the Mastery Check, 20 to 30 minutes per try, and submit the report. If a competency is not solid, go back to Study it for that competency, or to the second pass if you could not start it. Then retake the check.
  7. Use it. When every competency is solid, do the lab: PhysioEx, then your lab worksheet on what you learned and how it went. 2 to 3 hours, longer some weeks. You submit it in Canvas.
  8. Use it. Choose your application case and use the physiology. About 1 hour. You submit it in Canvas.
  9. Use it. Add this week's numbers to your patient's chart. About 30 minutes. You track this all term.
  10. Use it. Post in the discussion and reply to classmates. About 1 hour. You submit it in Canvas.
