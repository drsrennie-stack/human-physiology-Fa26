    Sep 14 2026: Week 2 is now cell junctions, not G protein signaling.
    Students pick one place in the body (small intestine lining, cardiac
    intercalated disc, or epidermis), predict which junction does the work
    there, then work it properly and trace one failure to a symptom. The
    post needs their hand drawn sketch of two neighboring cells with the
    junctions labeled; the instructions inside the frame say so, nothing
    extra is needed in the Canvas text.

    The second half is the metacognitive one, and it runs every week from
    here: how did your prediction do, what did the Mastery Check reveal,
    what did you do about it, what happened when you tried again. The Week 1
    Reflect discussion was a separate topic because Week 1 also had the
    vision board. From Week 2 there is one discussion carrying both halves.

    The full prompt is also written into the Canvas page at Week 2, Step 8,
    so a student never has to open the frame to know what to write. Both
    come from the same block in bio005-discussions.js, so they cannot drift.
