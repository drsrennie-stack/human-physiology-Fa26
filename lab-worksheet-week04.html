Week 4 lab worksheet

PhysioEx Exercise 3: Neurophysiology of Nerve Impulses, Activities 1 to 9. About 2 to 3 hours with the simulation.

Do each activity in PhysioEx first, then fill in its three boxes here while it is fresh. Write in your own words, in full sentences, and use your actual numbers.

What goes in each box

  1. What did you measure? Name what you changed and what you recorded, with units. For example, the stimulus voltage you set and the membrane potential you read.
  2. What were your findings? Give the actual results, with numbers. What went up, what went down, and what stayed the same.
  3. What did you learn? Explain why the results came out that way, using the physiology from this week. This is the most important box.

Your worksheet is graded once PhysioEx shows each activity complete.
