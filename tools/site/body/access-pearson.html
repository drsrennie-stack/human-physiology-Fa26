What you are buying, and why it is one purchase

There is a single required product for BIO 005. Buy it through Canvas, not from a reseller.

Pearson Mastering A&P, with PhysioEx, for Silverthorn.

The textbook comes with it. Our book is Human Physiology: An Integrated Approach by Dee Unglaub Silverthorn, 9th edition, and the eText is included in the Mastering purchase at no extra cost. You do not need to buy a separate book.

PhysioEx, the simulated lab software several of our labs run on, is part of the same package. So is the homework platform. One purchase covers the reading, the labs and the practice.

If you would rather also have a printed copy, that is your choice and it is an extra cost. Nothing in this course requires paper.

Buy it through Canvas. Here is the whole path.

Five steps, about ten minutes. Do this on a laptop or desktop if you can. It works on a phone, but the payment screens are easier to read on a bigger screen.

  1. Open our Canvas course and find Access Pearson. It is in the menu down the left side of the course, between Grades and NetTutor. If you do not see it, you are probably looking at your Canvas dashboard rather than inside BIO 005. Click the course card first, then look again.
  2. Sign in to Pearson, or make an account. If you have used a Pearson product in any class before, sign in with that same account. Do not make a second one. Two accounts is the most common reason a student pays and still cannot get in, because the money lands on the account they are not signed in to. If you have never used Pearson, create the account here, with an email address you will still be checking in December.
  3. Let it link your Canvas and Pearson accounts. You will be asked to connect the two. Say yes. This is what tells Pearson which course your work belongs to, and it is why buying through this screen registers you automatically. Sometimes it links itself and you will not be asked at all.
  4. Choose how you are paying. You get three options on this screen. All three are described below, so pick whichever one fits and keep going.
  5. Click through to the course and check that it worked. You should land on a card that says BIOL-5-D9286-2026FA, course ID rennie23265, Mastering A&P, September 8 to December 18, 2026, with my name on it. Open the eText once while you are there, so you know you can. If it drops you somewhere that does not name this course, stop and email me before you buy anything else.

The three options on the payment screen

They all end in the same course. The only difference is how you are paying.

Buy access online

Pay Pearson directly with a card or PayPal at that screen. This is the shortest route and the one most students take. The price you see there is the price for our exact package.

Redeem an access code

If you bought a code through the Yuba College bookstore, or your financial aid covers materials through the bookstore, you enter that code here. A code by itself does nothing until it is redeemed on this screen, so do not skip the earlier steps.

Temporary access, 14 days, no payment

If you cannot pay this week, take it. You get full access for 14 days and you start the course on time. Everything you do during those days is saved, and when you pay later, all of that work comes back with you.

Two things to know. You can use it once per course, so it is not a way to stretch the term out. And if the 14 days run out with no payment, you lose access until you pay, which partway through the Weeks 2 and 3 block is a bad place to be. Set a reminder on day 10.

Go to mlm.pearson.com/enrollment/rennie23265 (opens in a new tab). Sign in with your Pearson account or create one, then pick from the same three options above: enter a code, buy access, or take the 14 days. Then open BIOL-5-D9286-2026FA from My Courses.

Our course ID is rennie23265. If you ever call Pearson support, that is the number they will ask you for, so it is worth saving.

Please do not buy a code from a reseller

Access codes sold on Amazon, eBay, resale sites and textbook marketplaces are frequently for the wrong product, already used, or for a different edition. They usually cannot be refunded, and Pearson support cannot make them work for our course. Every term a few students lose real money this way, and I cannot get it back for them.

The Access Pearson screen inside our Canvas course gives you the right package at the right price, attached to the right section, in one step. Buy it there, or buy a code at the campus bookstore and redeem it there.

When something goes wrong

I paid, and Mastering says I have no access.

You are almost certainly signed in to a second Pearson account. Sign out completely, then sign back in with the account you used to pay. If you are not sure which one that is, search your email for the Pearson receipt and use that address.

Access Pearson is not in my Canvas menu.

Make sure you are inside the BIO 005 course and not on the dashboard. If it is still missing, email me and tell me which browser you are using. That one is on my end, not yours.

I got into Mastering but it is not our course.

Do not buy anything else. Email me with a screenshot of what you are seeing. This usually means the account linking in step three attached to an old course, and it is a quick fix.

My financial aid has not come through yet.

Take the 14 days of temporary access today and start on time. Then come talk to me. There is no version of this where you fall behind over money, and I would much rather hear about it now than in October.

PhysioEx will not open.

PhysioEx runs inside Mastering, so first confirm you can open the eText. If the eText opens and PhysioEx does not, try a different browser before anything else. Chrome and Firefox are the safest.

Still stuck?

Pearson runs its own support at support.pearson.com (opens in a new tab), and they are the right people for payment and account problems. For anything about which course you are in or what is due, come to me instead. Email srennie@yccd.edu or bring it to office hours.
