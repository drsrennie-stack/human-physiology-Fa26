## 4. Keyboard navigation

Verified in headless Chromium: Tab reaches Present, Reset, Print, every reveal button, every clinical bar, every sorter button, every figure (Enter or Space opens the lightbox, Escape closes and returns focus). In present mode, Arrow Right and Left, Page Up and Down, and Escape work. The pen layer keeps its shortcuts. Reveal boxes and clinical bars use `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. Slide counter and reset confirmation are `aria-live="polite"`.

Sorting activities (worked problems 4 and 5): each row is a table row with a row header, three or five `aria-pressed` buttons, and a result cell that is `role="status"` with `aria-live="polite"`, so the "Correct" or "Not quite" line is announced when it appears. A locked row cannot be changed until Reset. The per-table count line reads "n of 6 sorted". Result is given in words, never by color alone.

On the notes page: skip link, one h1, h2 per section, h5 inside worked problems; term lists are `dl` elements; tables carry captions and header scope.

## 5. Screen reader

Checked against the accessibility tree in Chromium (Playwright). Every Pearson figure has a full `alt` text written from the figure, not a filename, and a visible caption naming the figure number. Figure 3.9a (the five epithelia table) and figure 3.8a (the junction map) are described in the alt text row by row so problems 4 and 5 are answerable without sight. Worked steps are an ordered list with the numeral generated by CSS. Chemical formulas use `<sub>` and `<sup>` in visible text; alt text keeps formulas plain.

Not yet done: a manual pass with VoiceOver and NVDA. Same open item as the earlier decks.

## 6. Known limitations and remediation

- Figure images are raster, exported at up to 1700 px on the long side, and open in a lightbox at up to 94vw.
- Present mode auto-fit: at 1920 by 1080 no slide falls below 72 percent (slides 25 and 30). At 1600 by 900 the densest slides (25, 30, 35) reach 60 percent. Record at 1080p or larger. If any slide reads small on camera, the split points are: 25 (figure to its own slide), 35 (micrographs to their own slide).
- The site navigation injected by `bio005-nav.js` overlaps the slide header in present mode (inherited from the shell).
- Video captions cannot be verified until the lecture is recorded.
