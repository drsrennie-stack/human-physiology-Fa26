    Save a copy every day. If you do not, you risk losing your count.

    • Your count is saved only in this browser, on this device.
    • If you switch to another device or clear your browser, the count starts over, and you could lose the points.
    • After every session, press the report button below and save the report as a PDF, or take a picture of it. Each report shows the date and time you saved it, the dates you worked, and the topic of your cards.

    • If your count records properly, you only upload the one page report for the topic when you finish it.

    Settings

    Cards per session
    New cards a day

    A session is at least 20 cards and stops at the number you pick, so you can do one and come back later. The cards that were due and did not fit wait for next time, most overdue first. New cards a day stops the pile from growing faster than you can carry it. The topic list follows the course order. Choose "Through" for your daily review, because earlier topics keep coming back until you have proved them. Choose "only" when you want to focus on one topic, such as the one you are in or one you are weak on. Your cards and progress stay the same either way.

    The next seven days

    Reviews already scheduled. New cards are not counted here.

    How far up the ladder you are

    Every competency has three levels of card. Level 2 opens once you get two level 1 cards right on two different days, and level 3 opens the same way off level 2. You will not see every level 1 card. Once level 2 opens, you move on. You do not choose the level. It rises when you have earned it.

    Covering the course, not sampling it

    Your grade for each topic comes from how many cards you answer in that topic, with at least 70% right the first time. The levels at the top of the page show how many cards earns 70, 80, 90 or 100%. A session is at least 20 cards, about 8 minutes.

    What matters most is coming back on different days, because a level only opens when you get cards right on two different days. Three sessions on one night count as one day. Missing a day does not reset anything. The cards simply wait for you.

    Going back to earlier topics is automatic. A card from an earlier topic shows up again when it is due, so you keep what you learned.
