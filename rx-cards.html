    Settings

    New cards a day

    Reviews are never limited. Only new cards are, so the pile stays the size you can carry. The week list follows the course calendar: earlier weeks stay in, because their cards keep coming back until you have proved them.

    The next seven days

    Reviews already scheduled. New cards are not counted here.

    How far up the ladder you are

    Every competency has three levels of card. Level 2 opens once you have proved three level 1 cards; level 3 opens the same way. You do not choose the level. It rises when you have earned it.

    Covering the course, not sampling it

    Getting every competency to level 1 by the end of the term takes about 60 cards and 25 minutes a day, seven days a week. Days a week matter more than session length, because a card cannot move forward twice in one day. Five long sessions do less than seven short ones.

    Going back to earlier weeks is automatic. There is no chapter menu to manage; a card from Week 3 simply shows up in Week 9 when it is due.
