Everything goes to the root of human-physiology-Fa26 (keep the li/ folder structure). New or replaced files only.

WHAT IS HERE
  learning-guide.html, your-week.html, bio005-part2-data.js
      The one entry point, in the anatomy Learning Guide format with physiology content. Six sections:
      Learn it (note sheet to turn in, lecture video, click-through slides, course notes, competency prompts),
      Practice (Rx Cards graded by weekly count), The lab (clinical lab), The discussion, Check (Mastery Check,
      randomized practice exam), Help. Links not yet live show as "not posted yet"; to turn one on, paste the
      file name or Canvas link into bio005-part2-data.js.
  rx-cards.html
      Adds the weekly card report: name box, running count against the bands (70 D, 105 C, 175 B, 350 A),
      "Print this week's report" (one stamped page with a report code), and an optional automatic post of each
      day's count to a Google Sheet you own. The sheet is off until you paste a URL; see rx-cards-sheet-setup.txt.
  cardiac-lab.html (Week 9) and hemodynamics-lab.html (Week 10)
      The clinical labs. Each calculation case is a question, not a list of fields: the student picks formulas from
      the sticky column on the right, drags the numbers into slots, Calculate checks the slot holds the right number
      and reads the result against the reference range, and the interpretation locks in only once everything the
      question needs has been worked (without saying what is missing). Print to PDF, upload.
  discussion-w9-engineering.html
      The Week 9 engineering-team discussion: brief, five targets, the three-part post, the two replies, rubric.
  li/w9/ and li/w10/
      The click-through study pages and the student lecture note sheet PDFs the guide links to.
  compliance-notes-part2.md
  NOT-FOR-THE-REPO-instructor-keys/   the two lab keys. Do not push.
