WHY THE NUMBERS LOOKED WRONG
I kept saying "43 commits waiting" while handing you zips of eight or ten
files. Those count different things. A commit is one change I made; a zip
held only the files that changed since the last zip, which is how you asked
for them. Forty-two commits touched 196 files in total, and until now you
had them in pieces. That was a confusing way to report it and I should have
given you a running total of files instead.
