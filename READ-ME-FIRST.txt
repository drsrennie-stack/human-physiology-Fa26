What is here
  instructor/BIO005-W09-Slides-Heart-as-a-Pump.html   the deck. Self-contained, open it in a browser, press Present.
                                                       Built from the 21 Week 9 competencies in bio005-competencies.csv, in teaching order.
                                                       Under every slide header: "You should be able to" with the competency statement(s)
                                                       that slide teaches, word for word from bio005-competencies.csv. Same line sits under
                                                       each section heading on the sheet.
                                                       Seven sections (A to G), 45 slides per class set. Every drawing gets two slides:
                                                         1. label the drawing: "What you are looking at" first, then the pins, one per click
                                                         2. how it happens: the same drawing, the steps in order as gold markers joined by a
                                                            gold flow line, one per click. Each step carries a close-up picture of that moment
                                                            (ions moving, the channel opening, the valve event) and says what happens; the last
                                                            click is "The effect: what changes because of this"
                                                       Then, where the week needs them:
                                                         steps in order (one step per click, click a step for the detail)
                                                         mini mind map (one branch per click)
                                                         fill in / fill the table (one line or cell per click)
                                                       Clickers: one concept question after each section (30 s, confidence points) and five
                                                       label-it questions through the deck (15 s, one tap). Class sets A, B, C on the title bar;
                                                       rooms HEART9, HEART9B, HEART9C.
