Drawings (all original, muted so the pins are the exercise): one circuit with the pressure profile, the arterial pressure wave with a cuff
being let down, one capillary with the Starling forces, venous return (thorax, leg vein, venoconstriction), the baroreceptor reflex,
the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system with ADH and ANP, and the adrenal gland in section. Each gets a label slide and a
how-it-happens slide with step close-ups. Mind maps with branch drawings: how an arteriole decides its own tone, four ways to get
edema, and the hormones that hold pressure over days. Tables: the adrenal cortex zones, and what changes in exercise. Steps:
compensation in hemorrhage and where it fails.
