Your second pass, step by step

You have already filled these boxes once from the reading. This time you are adding what the videos give you that the book did not, in a different color, so you can see the difference later.

  1. Get out the Competency Study Guide you already worked from the reading, with your first pass on it.
  2. Switch to a second color, and stay in that color for everything you add from here.
  3. Press play on the first video and let the week run, or click the concept in the list that matches the box you are working on.
  4. When a video gives you something the reading did not, add it to that same box. Do not start a fresh sheet.
  5. Leave the gaps where they are. A thin box is not a mistake, it is the sheet telling you where to go back.
  6. At the end, look at how much of the sheet is in the second color. That is exactly where your reading was thin, and it is what you study first.
