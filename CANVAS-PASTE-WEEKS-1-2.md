# Canvas paste guide: Weeks 1 and 2

Every page in both modules, in order. Each one is a Canvas Page: create it, give
it the title in the first column, switch the editor to the `</>` HTML view, paste
the block, save, then add it to the module.

The iframe heights are measured from the rendered pages. Canvas strips `<script>`
from a pasted page, so the height has to be fixed and the student scrolls inside
the frame. The plain link under each frame is the fallback for devices that block
third-party iframes, which is most school-managed iPads and Safari in low power
mode.

Week 1 and Week 2 both run eight steps. The course introduction video is gone,
and Print your week and First pass are now one page.

Sep 15 2026: every Week 2 due date is Sunday, September 27. Weeks 2 and 3 are one
stretch with nothing due in between.
