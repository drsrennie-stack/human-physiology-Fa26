What this is

One box for every competency in Week 1. You build it by hand, on paper, in two passes and two colours. It is the first thing you do each week and the last thing you should skip, because it is also your study guide when the midterm comes.

Due Sunday at 11:59 pm, the Sunday that closes Week 1. Up to 24 hours late is an automatic 50 percent reduction. After 24 hours it is a zero.

Do it in this order

  1. Read this week's competencies firstOpen the competency list and read the Week 1 entries. Those are the boxes on your sheet, and they are the exact things you have to be able to do.
  2. First pass, in your first colourWork through the sheet using your textbook and your own notes, and nothing else. Do not open the videos yet. Organize what you can already put together: mini mind maps, lists, sequences, arrows. Keep it short.
  3. Stop while the boxes are still half emptyThis is deliberate. You are meant to arrive at the videos already holding a partly built picture, so that watching fills gaps you can actually feel instead of washing over you.
  4. Now watch this week's concept videosWatch them the whole way through, with your sheet in front of you.
  5. Second pass, in a different colourGo back through the same boxes and add what you were missing, correct what you had wrong, and write down whatever finally made sense once you heard it explained.
  6. Do not erase the first passLeave it visible underneath, mistakes and all. Those wrong marks are the most useful thing on the page, for you and for me.

Why two colours

Read this part

The two colours are how I can tell you watched

When I read your sheet I can see exactly which parts of your understanding came from your reading and which came from the videos. That tells me something no score can.

A sheet that arrives in a single colour has not finished the assignment, whatever is written on it, because it tells me the videos were skipped or the whole thing was done in one sitting afterwards. If that happens, go back, watch, and add your second pass.

This is also the honest way for you to find out what you understood on your own before anyone explained it to you. That is precisely what your midterm asks about.

How to photograph and submit it

Your two colours only work if they survive the photo. Take thirty seconds over this and you will not have to redo it.

  • Colour photo or colour scan only. Greyscale or black-and-white scans erase the entire point of the assignment and cannot be graded.
  • Good light, no shadow across the page. Daylight near a window beats a ceiling light directly overhead. Your own shadow falling across the sheet is the most common problem.
  • Square to the page. Hold your phone flat above the sheet rather than at an angle, so the box edges stay straight and the writing stays readable.
  • Every page, in order, lecture journal then lab journal. Check each image before you upload it: if you cannot read your own handwriting on screen, neither can I.
  • One PDF if you can. Most phone camera apps will combine pages into a single PDF. Separate image files are fine too, just name them so the order is obvious.

If a photo comes out unreadable I will ask you to resubmit, and the late policy still applies, so check before you upload rather than after.
