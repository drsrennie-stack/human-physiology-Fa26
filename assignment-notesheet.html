What happens to it

Nothing is scored. The sheet is yours, and it is what your recall cards, your lab work and your exam review are built from. You do upload it: once both passes are on it, photograph or scan every page and turn it in as one file in the Canvas note sheet assignment for the week, by Sunday at 10:00 pm Pacific. I mark it complete or not complete and read enough to see how the week went for you. Turning it in every week is how you show you are participating in the class, and participating is a condition of staying enrolled.

  1. Handwritten, on paper, in your two colors, because the gap between the colors is the whole point and it disappears the moment you type it.
  2. Label every box with the competency number and name, so you can find something in week 12 without reading all fourteen pages.
  3. Photograph each page as you finish it, in good light, flat on the table, so the writing in both colors can be read. Losing the sheet costs you no points and a great deal of work.
  4. Bring it to office hours any time you want me to look at it with you. It is a good use of the hour.
