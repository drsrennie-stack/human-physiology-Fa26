Three ways to practice

Pick one and start. They all do the same job, which is getting the material back out of your head without looking, and the one you do today beats the one you meant to do.

Back to Canvas modules

  • Rx Cards

    Spaced cards

    The ones you miss come back tomorrow. The ones you know come back later.

    12 min, opens in a new tab

  • Brain Dump

    From memory, then check

    Spin a prompt, draw it on paper with nothing open, then tick off what you left out.

    20 min, opens in a new tab

  • Book Problems

    Work it, then work it backward

    Try it before you look. Then start from the answer and see how the author got there.

    15 min, opens in a new tab

None of this is graded

All three are practice. Nothing here goes in the gradebook, and nothing here is checked. The point is to find out what you cannot do yet while it is still cheap to find out.

Rx Cards and Brain Dump keep track of your progress, so they open in their own browser tab. Close the tab and you are back here.
