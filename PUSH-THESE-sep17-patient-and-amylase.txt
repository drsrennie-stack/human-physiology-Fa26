1. THE NEW PAGE: patient-how-this-works.html
=======================================================================
A single page that answers the three things students are actually stuck on. It
is built on the step page template, so it iframes into Canvas like the others.

  a. The two things with "patient" in the name, and which one gets turned in.
     The week's case, every Sunday, Use It 20 percent. The chart, once, on
     December 16, Use It 5 percent. Said plainly, twice.

  b. What "draw this week's control loop by hand" means. This is the one
     Carlton asked about and nothing on the site explained it. Six labelled
     boxes in a circle, worked through with Camila running in the heat: the
     number being held steady, what pushed it off, the sensor, the integrating
     center, the output and effector, the response and the arrow back. Then
     label it negative or positive and say how you know. It ends with the test
     of whether they have it: hand the page to somebody not in the class and
     they should be able to name the number being defended.

  c. Where the AI log goes. At the bottom of the same chart page, one or two
     lines. If they used none, write None.

  It also says in as many words that the disabled page was a button that should
  not have existed, that there was nothing behind it, and that they have not
  missed a deadline. Plus step by step for Week 1 and Week 2, why it is built
  this way, and a quick answers section that ends by telling a student who feels
  behind that they are not.
