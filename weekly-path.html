The seven steps, in order

Understand it, then use it, then find out what stuck. Every week of this course runs the same way.

  1. 1

    Start here

    Open the weekly overview

    • Read what the week is for
    • See what you will be learning
    • Check what is due and when
    • Look at the whole path before you start

    Know where you are going.

  2. 2

    Learn

    Learn the physiology

    • Read the assigned chapter or the written notes
    • Fill your note sheet in your first color
    • Watch the concept videos
    • Go back in a second color and add what changed

    Build your foundation.

  3. 3

    Practice retrieval

    Pull it back out of your head

    • Do a brain dump with nothing open
    • Run your recall cards
    • Work through practice questions
    • Draw a diagram from memory, then check it

    Strengthen your recall.

  4. 4

    Check yourself

    Find out what you actually know

    • Take the Mastery Check
    • See what you can explain with no help
    • Read what the Gap Finder names
    • Go back to the material for anything you missed

    Know your gaps.

  5. 5

    Apply

    Use the physiology

    • Work through your patient case
    • Analyze data or lab results
    • Solve a physiology problem
    • Read a figure or a graph and say what it means

    Move from knowing to doing.

  6. 6

    Submit

    Turn in the required work

    • Upload your note sheet
    • Turn in the lab and the case
    • Post your discussions and reply
    • Check that nothing marked Submit is still open
    Submit

    Show what you have learned.

  7. 7

    Close the loop

    Finish the week properly

    • Confirm everything is actually submitted
    • Look at your Gap Finder one more time
    • Pick two or three things to keep practicing
    • Put a retrieval session later in the week on your calendar

    Make it stick.

  • Do not skip steps

    The assignments assume you have already learned the material and practiced getting it back. Skipping a step makes the rest of the week feel confusing and much harder than it needs to be.

  • Not everything is a submission

    Most of what you do in a week is learning, practice and self checks, and none of it is graded. The things you have to turn in are marked with the Submit icon. Look for that icon.

  • Need help?

    If you ever feel lost, go back to the weekly overview and start again at Step 1. You can also message me in the Canvas Inbox or ask in the Virtual Office, where your classmates can see the answer too.

Hard work here builds a stronger you.
