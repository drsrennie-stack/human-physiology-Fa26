BIO 005 Human Physiology · Weeks 10 to 15

Choose your mastery pathway

From Week 10 on, you pick a target level on every drawing, case, lab, project and Mastery Check.

  • 70 Foundational
  • 80 Mechanistic
  • 90 Applied
  • 100 Integrated

Why this change

You choose your goal. What you show sets your grade.

You came to this course with different goals. Some of you need a C for a degree or program. Others need an A or a B to get into a competitive program. For Weeks 10 to 15, you choose the level you are working toward on each assignment, and your score is the level your work shows.

Step 1

Pick your target on each assignment

The four target levels
TargetWhat your work shows
70%FoundationalYou draw, identify and explain the essential mechanisms accurately.
80%MechanisticAll of the above, plus cause and effect, including how the body regulates it.
90%AppliedAll of the above, plus you predict what happens when something is disturbed, with sound reasoning.
100%IntegratedAll of the above, plus you analyze new, complex cases that involve more than one body system.
  • The levels build on each other. Aiming for 90% means you also show everything for 70% and 80%.
  • You can pick a different target on every assignment.
  • 70% is a real choice. It still has to be accurate and complete.

Step 2

Which assignments use a target

  • A drawing for each lecture. Normal, disrupted, and the fix, drawn by hand.
  • Two cases for each topic. Shorter versions of the midterm cases.
  • The lab. PhysioEx or one of my labs.
  • The Mastery Check. You pick your target before you start, and you get the questions for that level and every level below it.
  • The projects. For example, the Wiggers diagram, the GI map and the renal map.

Each assignment lists exactly what it needs at 70, 80, 90 and 100 percent. The lecture note sheet and Rx Cards are recommended, not required. Midterm 2 keeps the Midterm 1 format: two cases, 50 points each.

Step 3

How your score is set

  • Your score is the highest level your work shows, not the target you picked.
  • Aim for 90% and show 90%: you earn 90%.
  • Aim for 90% and show 80%: you earn 80%.
  • Aim for 70% and your work shows more: you earn what it shows. The one exception is the Mastery Check, where the questions stop at your target.
  • Work that does not reach 70% is scored on how much of the foundational work is accurate and complete. Missing or very inaccurate work can earn a zero.

On the Mastery Check, a level counts when you get at least 80 percent of its questions right, and every level below it counts too. Your score is the highest level that counts. If the 70 level does not count, your score is your percent correct on the foundational questions, up to 69 percent.

Step 4

One revision

Every assignment has two graded attempts.

  1. Your first submission. Do everything for the target you picked.
  2. One revision. Fix what was wrong, add what was missing, explain it better, or go for a higher level. A revision can earn up to 90%, whatever target you pick. On a Mastery Check, your second attempt is a different set of questions on the same competencies.
  • Aim for 70% to get through the unit, then revise to move up, as high as 90%.
  • Aim for 100% and show 80%: revise, and show everything listed for 80% and 90% to earn 90%.

You do not need to redo work that was already accurate.

Last thing each week

The Know-It Check

  • 12 questions in Canvas on that week's material, three at each level. About 15 minutes.
  • Required. Answer every question and submit, and it is done. Your score does not count toward your grade.
  • Do it last, with everything closed, so it shows what you know on your own.
  • After each question, mark how sure you were. At the end, tell me what is still unclear.

I read every check. Each week I post what the class missed and reteach it, and I write back to anyone who asks a question or seems stuck.

One unit at a time

Moving to the next unit

  • Inside a unit, turn things in in any order.
  • Score at least 70% on each assignment to open the next unit. The Know-It Check only needs to be done.
  • Under 70%? Use my feedback, fix it, and turn it in again. That is your one revision, worth up to 90%.
  • Still under 70% after your revision? I will send you a short video on what is missing. Your score stands, and I will open the next unit for you.
  • The next unit opens once your work is graded. Plan for a short wait.
  • Turn in real work. A blank file, a placeholder, or work turned in just to open the next unit earns a zero and uses your first attempt.

On every assignment

What does not change

  • Drawings do not need to be artistic. They need to be accurate and show how the mechanism works.
  • Follow the resource and AI rules on each assignment. The Mastery Check is done with everything closed.
  • On the Mastery Check you may see new data or a new patient. Show that you understand the physiology, not that you remember an answer.
