## Coverage check

**All 26 chapters used. Nothing in the book is orphaned, nothing in your schedule is unsupported.**

Three chapters are read across two weeks, each time for a different half. All three splits are deliberate:

- **Ch 6** splits between Week 1 (homeostasis and feedback) and Week 4 (cell signaling), matching your decision to merge cell communication into the signaling week rather than teaching it as its own topic.
- **Ch 9** splits between Week 4 (the neuron and the action potential) and Week 5 (the synapse).
- **Ch 12** splits between Week 6 (autonomic) and Week 7 (somatic motor control), so the autonomic half sits with the sensory input it responds to and the somatic half sits with the muscle it commands.
