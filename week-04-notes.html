What this week covers

Work through the topics in order, because each one uses the one before it. Each topic has three parts that go together: the walkthrough, which is the lecture; a worksheet you print and fill in while you work through it, writing each prediction before you press Show me; and written notes to study from afterward, in bullets, definitions and sequences.

  1. Neurons and neuroglia. The map of the nervous system, neuron shapes and regions, the six glial cells, myelin and demyelination, and axonal transport. Week 4 competencies 7 to 11.
    The walkthrough · the written notes · notes to print (PDF) (opens in a new tab)
  2. The resting membrane potential. Where the ions are, how the inside becomes negative, equilibrium potentials, the Nernst and Goldman equations in plain words, the pump, and blood potassium. Week 4 competencies 1 to 5.
    The walkthrough · the written notes · notes to print (PDF) (opens in a new tab)
  3. Ion channel gating. Leak and gated channels, what opens a gate, the two gates and three states of the voltage-gated sodium channel, the slow potassium channel, and patients.
    The walkthrough · the written notes · notes to print (PDF) (opens in a new tab)
  4. Graded potentials. How a graded potential is made, why it fades as it spreads, summation, and threshold at the trigger zone. Week 4 competencies 12 and 14.
    The walkthrough · the written notes · notes to print (PDF) (opens in a new tab)
  5. The action potential. Threshold and positive feedback, the phases and the channels behind each one, all or none, how stimulus strength is coded, refractory periods, and a patient on a potassium channel blocker. Week 4 competencies 13 to 16.
    The walkthrough · the written notes · notes to print (PDF) (opens in a new tab)
  6. How action potentials carry information. Walkthrough coming. For now, the lecture notes: read the notes · notes to print (PDF) (opens in a new tab)
  7. The chemical synapse. Walkthrough coming. For now, the lecture notes: read the notes · notes to print (PDF) (opens in a new tab)
  8. Integration at the synapse. Walkthrough coming. For now, the lecture notes: read the notes · notes to print (PDF) (opens in a new tab)
