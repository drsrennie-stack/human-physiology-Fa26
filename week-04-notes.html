Membrane potential, neurons and synapses

The written notes for this week. Outline is final, the teaching prose posts when the week opens.

What this week covers

Membrane potential, neurons and synapses. The week is taught in six lectures. Work through them in order, because each one uses the one before it. Every lecture has two levels. The story is the easy first pass: one small step at a time, a drawing to add to, and no numbers. The full lecture then adds the patient, the numbers and the worked problems. Every notes page follows its lecture slide by slide, has the worked problems written out in full, and has a moving model near the top that you can run yourself.

  1. The resting membrane potential. Build the voltage from an empty cell: the pump, potassium leak channels, the Nernst and Goldman equations, and what blood potassium does to every cell. Includes a moving model of the ions and four worked problems. Week 4 competencies 1 to 5. Start with the story and its draw along sheet. Then read the notes or open the slides.
  2. Neurons and glia. Neuron classes, the four working regions of a neuron and the channels in each, the six glial cells, myelin and what happens when it is lost, and axonal transport with real times. Includes a moving model of transport along an axon. Week 4 competencies 7 to 11. Start with the story and its draw along sheet. Then read the notes or open the slides.
  3. Graded potentials and the action potential. Graded potentials, the two gates of the voltage gated sodium channel built one step at a time, threshold, every phase of the action potential, and all or none. Includes a model that fires an action potential. Week 4 competencies 12 to 14. Start with the story and its draw along sheet. Then read the notes or open the slides.
  4. How action potentials carry information. Refractory periods, frequency coding, conduction and conduction velocity, myelin loss, and what potassium, calcium and local anesthetics do to excitability. Includes a model that races two axons. Week 4 competencies 15 to 18. Start with the story and its draw along sheet. Then read the notes or open the slides.
  5. The chemical synapse. The nine steps at a chemical synapse in order, fast and slow receptors, the seven classes of neurotransmitter, and the three ways a signal is ended. Includes a model of one working synapse. Week 4 competencies 21 to 23. Start with the story and its draw along sheet. Then read the notes or open the slides.
  6. Integration at the synapse. EPSPs and IPSPs, summation over time and space with numbers, presynaptic inhibition, long term potentiation, and electrical synapses. Includes a model that asks whether the neuron will fire. Week 4 competencies 24 to 28. Start with the story and its draw along sheet. Then read the notes or open the slides.

Competencies 6, 19, 20 and 29 are the simulations and the nerve conduction measurement, and they are done in lab. The full list with both prompts for each competency is on the Week 4 competency list.
