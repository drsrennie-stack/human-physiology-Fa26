  • It has to be posted here and open to everyone. A private session with two friends is a fine thing to do, it just does not earn points.

How hosting works.
  • Host on your own Zoom, Meet or Discord. Record it. That recording is what earns your host hour.
  • After the session, tap "Host? Add recording and attendance" and paste your recording link and Zoom attendance report.
  • Post a door contact and watch it. Every session carries one contact for people who cannot get in. Keep it open on your phone or your screen from 10 minutes before the start until the session ends, and answer it. If somebody took the time to show up, they get in.
  • Bring a cohost for anything big. One of you runs the room, the other watches the door and the messages, so nobody sits outside waiting while you are mid-sentence. On Zoom, make them a cohost so they can admit people themselves.
  • Test your meeting link before you post it, using the test button in the form. A dead link is the one thing that wastes everybody's hour.
  • Sign up with the name on the roster, and use it in the meeting, so hosts can match you.
  • Your sign-up email stays private to Dr. Rennie. The only thing posted publicly is the door contact you choose when you host, and a phone number is never required. Email or Discord is fine if you would rather not give out your number.
  • If you sign up, show up, or cancel your seat so someone else can take it.
