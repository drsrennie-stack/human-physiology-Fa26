Before you start

When it runs. Week 1 runs Tuesday, September 8 through Sunday, September 13 at 10:00 pm Pacific. Everything in this module is this week, and only this week.

How long. Plan on about 12 hours, spread across the week rather than done in one sitting. That is the floor for passing, not the number that earns an A.

Your reading. Silverthorn Chapter 1, and the homeostasis and control pathway sections of Chapter 6.

The order you work in

Do not jump ahead. Every step assumes the one before it happened.

  1. 1
    Learn

    Print your week

    Tuesday, about 45 minutes plus printing

    Everything you work from this week, on paper: the note sheet, the competency list and the written notes. Print it before you start, so you are working with paper in front of you instead of switching windows.

  2. 2
    Learn

    Read it, then watch it

    Tuesday to Thursday, about 3 hours

    First pass in one color, from Silverthorn Chapter 1 or the written notes, filling each box on the note sheet as you go. Then switch colors, watch the concept videos, and add what the video gave you that the reading did not. Leave the gaps. The gaps are what tell you where to go back.

  3. 3
    Learn

    Upload your note sheet

    After your second pass, by Sunday

    Photograph every page with both colors on it and upload it in Canvas. I am not grading it. Turning it in is how you show you are participating.

    Graded: complete or not complete
  4. 4
    Practice

    Study it for several days

    Every day from Thursday, about an hour a day

    Now the note sheet closes and the material has to come back out of your head. Brain dumps, drawing from memory, recall cards, book problems. Pick more than one. Four short sessions beat one long one, because the forgetting in between is what makes it stick.

    Not graded, and the step that decides your exam score
  5. 5
    Apply

    Lab, the Reference Range Lab

    Wednesday to Sunday, about 3 hours

    Calculate a reference range, decide which results fall in or out of it, and plot three serial results by hand. Work the lab page top to bottom and turn it in as one file.

    Graded: Investigation
  6. 6
    Apply

    Your patient, the preseason physical

    Saturday, about 90 minutes

    One more entry in the chart you keep all term. Draw this week's mechanism by hand, answer the written questions, and log any AI you used.

    Graded: Application
  7. 7
    Apply

    Two discussions this week

    Discussion 1 by Friday, September 11; Discussion 2 by Sunday, September 13

    Your digital vision board introduces you to the class. The second post looks at what your Mastery Check told you and what you did about it, so take the check first.

    Graded: Thinking
  8. 8
    Check

    Mastery Check, and upload your report

    Saturday or Sunday, 30 to 60 minutes

    Thirty questions on this week's competencies, nothing open. It names the competencies that cost you points. Do one or do ten, then upload the report. The score is for you, not for a grade.

    Graded: complete or not complete
