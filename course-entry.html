Welcome to Human Physiology

This course is about how the body works. Not what the parts are called, but what they do, how they work together, and what happens when one of them stops working correctly.

Everything runs online, lecture and lab both, on your own schedule inside each week. Before you start, take five minutes to get your device set up so the course tools work the first time you open them.

How much time this really takes

BIOL 5 is a four unit lab course. Four units is about 216 hours of work for the semester. A normal term spreads those hours across 18 weeks. Ours runs 14 weeks, so the same 216 hours have to fit into fewer of them.

A typical week

Lababout 8 hours
Lecture materialabout 3 hours
Studying on your ownabout 5 hours
Every weekabout 15 to 16 hours

That is the minimum for a passing grade. It is not the number for an A, and it is not an estimate of how long the work takes if you are struggling with it.

Taking physiology online does not reduce any of that. It is the same course, the same 216 hours, and the same standard as the section that meets in a room. What changes is that you schedule the hours yourself instead of a room and a clock scheduling them for you. There is no version of this course that takes less time, and there is no shortcut that substitutes for the hours.

If 15 hours a week does not fit in your term right now, that is worth knowing this week rather than in November. Come talk to me and we will look at your options while you still have them.

What the work is

Where those hours go

  • You draw every week. Drawing a process by hand is how you show that you understand it. An answer key cannot draw one for you.
  • You get your own patient. Your term project is one patient file, seeded to you, that you build week by week and defend at the end.
  • There is no online final exam. The final is a synthesis project and a case conference.
  • You do not need anatomy first. Some of you have taken it and some have not. The course is built to work either way.

If a check fails

What to do about it

A check that says Needs attention means a setting on your device has to change. It does not block you from the course.

If you cannot get it working, message me in Canvas and tell me which line said Needs attention. That one detail is usually enough for me to send you the fix.
