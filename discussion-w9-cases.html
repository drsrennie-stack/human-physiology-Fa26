Present your two cases

Post your Wiggers case and your cardiac lab case in one post. You will not see classmates’ posts until you post your own.

For each case

  1. Your case number and twist number in the first line, for example “Wiggers Case 4, Twist 2.”
  2. Three photos of your drawing: about half done, finished before your twist, and after your twist redraw.
  3. Your explanation: a photo of your handwritten answers, or a short typed summary of them.

Then answer these about your own drawing

  1. Put a star on the spot that gave you the most trouble. What made it hard, and how did you work it out?
  2. Point to one mistake you crossed out. What did you first think, and what changed your mind?
  3. If you met this patient, what is the first thing you would check, and why?

Keep your crossed-out mistakes visible. They show your thinking, and they count in your favor.
