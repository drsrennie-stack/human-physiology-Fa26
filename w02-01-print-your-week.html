Monday, about 20 minutes plus printing

Week 2 comes in two halves, and each half has its own note sheet and its own competency list. Print all four before you start.

Work the halves in order. The anatomy is the structure, and the physiology is what that structure does, so the second half only makes sense once you can see where it is happening.

Foundations of the cell and tissues

The anatomy: organelles, the membrane itself, and the four tissue types. The note sheet has a box per competency with the prompts already on it. The competency list says what each box is asking you for, so keep it beside you while you fill them in.
