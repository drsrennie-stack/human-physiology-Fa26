Why this comes first

Before we talk physiology, it is important to me to know who you are, and for you to get to know each other. This class runs online, so we do not get the accidental version of this, the talking before lecture starts. We are doing it on purpose, in week one, before anything else.

This is your introduction. There is no separate introduce yourself post to do as well. Everything goes here.

This is Discussion 1 of Week 1, at Step 7 of the eight steps. It sits in the Canvas module and on this website with the same wording in both places.

What you are making

A vision board is a collage: several images arranged together so they read as one picture. Photos, cut outs, words, colors, whatever gets it across. Build it however you like, in Canva, in Google Slides, in a photo app, or on paper with scissors and glue and then photograph it. What you turn in is one image file of the finished collage.

Cover four things, weighted however you like.

  1. Who you are. Photos of yourself if you are comfortable, and only what you are happy for classmates to see.
  2. What matters to you. People, places, things you care about, what you do when you are not in class.
  3. Why you are taking this class. The real reason. It is required for my program is a real reason and you can say so.
  4. Where you are going. What you are working toward, in school or after it.

On privacy, and I mean this

Share only what you want classmates to have. Do not put your address, your workplace, your schedule, your phone number, or anything with a document number on it into the board.

If you would rather not include photos of yourself, use images that represent you instead. Nobody loses a point for that. If there is something you want me to know but not the class, message me in the Canvas Inbox instead of putting it on the board.

Three ways to build it

  • Pinterest. Build a board, arrange it, screenshot it. Send the screenshot, not the board link.
  • One slide. PowerPoint or Google Slides. Drop images and text on one slide, then export the slide as an image.
  • All video. Skip the image and make the whole thing as one video, showing and telling as you go.

How to post

  1. Attach your board image to the Week 1 discussion, with a short video of you introducing yourself and walking us through it. A phone video is perfect.
  2. Post by Friday, September 11, 10:00 pm Pacific, so there is someone to meet over the weekend.
  3. Reply to at least two classmates by Sunday, September 13, 10:00 pm Pacific. Real replies, about their board, not nice board.
